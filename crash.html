How this page stays live

Every number links to its source and carries its own date, and where a reading couldn't be pulled the card says so rather than guessing. The trip lines are watch lines I wrote for this page from the history shown, not trading rules. A few history lines are marked as coming from memory or from the project's earlier study, because the company that owns the junk-bond indexes only lets the Fed's site show three years of them.
