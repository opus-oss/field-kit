This is an estimate, not tax advice. It uses 2025 federal brackets and standard deductions and each state's 2025 income-tax schedule. It does not include tax credits (child, EITC, education), state-specific deductions or exemptions, local/city income taxes, the QBI deduction, capital-gains rates, AMT, or above-the-line adjustments. State figures for married-joint and head-of-household are approximated from single-filer schedules. Pre-tax retirement is treated as traditional (pre-tax) and HSA as a payroll contribution; a few states differ (PA taxes 401(k) contributions; CA and NJ tax HSA contributions) and that isn't modeled. Verify with a professional or the IRS/state site before filing.
