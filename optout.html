Opt Out

A data-removal workbook in the Bazzell tradition, with the tedious parts automated. Your profile, progress and notes live only in this browser. Nothing is sent anywhere; the only network traffic is the opt-out pages you choose to open.

Progress

The method order matters

0
Set up clean channels first

A masked email for opt-outs (SimpleLogin, addy.io or Firefox Relay), a VoIP number you can burn (MySudo, Google Voice), a password manager, and a fresh browser profile with no extensions. Brokers often verify by emailing the address they already have on you, so keep your old addresses reachable during the run.

1
Freeze the bureaus

Equifax, Experian, TransUnion, then the three everyone forgets: Innovis, ChexSystems, NCTUE. Free, permanent, and it stops most identity fraud cold. Thaw for a day when you actually apply for something.

2
Stop the firehose

OptOutPrescreen (do the permanent mailed form, not just the 5-year), DMAchoice, Catalog Choice, the Do Not Call registry, NAI and DAA ad opt-outs, the big-tech ad settings, and a GLBA opt-out letter to every bank and card issuer you hold. This is where the credit card mail and most of the calls come from.

3
Cut the upstream aggregators

LexisNexis, Acxiom, Epsilon, CoreLogic, Thomson Reuters CLEAR, TransUnion and the bureau marketing arms. These feed the people-search sites. Starve them and the sites below repopulate slower.

4
Sweep the people-search sites

Work by parent company. One PeopleConnect suppression covers Intelius, TruthFinder, Instant Checkmate, US Search and ZabaSearch. Most want the URL of your own profile, so use Find me first, then paste it.

5
Scrub the search engines

Google's Results About You tool removes results that show your home address, phone or email. Bing has a content-removal form. Do this last, after the source pages are gone, or the results come back.

6
Maintain

Data repopulates. Re-check people-search sites every 90 days and aggregators every 6 months. The tracker sets the dates for you and flags what is overdue. Re-run Find me each time.

Your legal lever

Set your state on the Profile tab. Every letter on the Letters tab cites the statute automatically. Statutes give you a right to delete and to opt out of sale, and put a response deadline on the broker. A broker that ignores a valid request has broken the law, and the escalation letter says so.

If you want it hands-off

A paid service (DeleteMe, Optery, Incogni) submits and re-submits for you. The trade is that you hand them your full profile, and they only cover the sites on their list. Doing it yourself with this workbook covers more, costs nothing, and keeps your data on your own device. Bazzell's free workbook is the canonical source if a link here has moved: inteltechniques.com/workbook.

Beyond opt-outs Extreme Privacy tier

Opt-outs treat symptoms. The cause is your name on records. The next level is a private mailbox (PMB) as your address of record everywhere, a trust or LLC holding the house so the deed is not in your name, utilities and phone in a company or alias name where legal, and never using your real address for anything that publishes it. That is a project, not a checklist, and it is worth it before the next home purchase, not after.
